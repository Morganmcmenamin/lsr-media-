# LSR Media — Google Ads Launch Checklist

*A step-by-step guide for getting the most out of the $600 Google Ads credit. Work through the phases in order — Phase 1 is the part most first-timers skip, and it's the part that decides whether the $600 is money well spent or money wasted.*

## What to expect (read this first)

- Think of the $600 as a **test, not a money machine.** Its real job is to find the 2–3 search terms that actually produce bookings, so any future spend is money you spend with confidence.
- **Honest expectation: 2–5 real bookings** from the $600. At LSR's prices ($175–$980) that's roughly $800–$1,500 of work from a free credit — plus a list of proven keywords. Anyone promising 10+ bookings is overselling.
- **The single most important rule: don't spend a dollar until tracking works (Phase 1).** Without it, you can't tell which ad produced a booking — the money is gone with nothing learned.

---

## Phase 1 — Before spending a single dollar (the non-negotiable gate)

**Conversion tracking** *(so Google can see which clicks become bookings)*
- [ ] Create a Google Ads account and a free Google Analytics (GA4) property.
- [ ] In Google Ads, create a conversion action called **"Booking Confirmed"** and a second one called **"Phone Call."** Copy down the `AW-XXXXXXXXXX` ID and conversion label for each. *(Hand these to your developer to install.)*
- [ ] Have the tracking code installed on the **"Booking Confirmed" thank-you page** and across the site. *(The thank-you page already exists with a placeholder — it just needs the real code.)*
- [ ] **Cal.com setting (important & easy to miss):** in your Cal.com dashboard, set every booking event type to **redirect to `lsrmedia.co.nz/thank-you.html` after booking.** The booking finishes on Cal.com's site, so without this redirect Google never learns the booking happened.
- [ ] **Commercial quotes:** set up a confirmation page for the quote form too, so quote requests get counted. *(Currently they aren't.)*
- [ ] **TEST IT:** make one fake booking start-to-finish and confirm it shows up in Google Ads before going live. *This is the step that makes or breaks the whole campaign.*

**Landing page speed** *(faster page = lower cost per click)*
- [ ] Compress the portfolio photos (some are 8–11MB; they should be under 300KB each). *Does not change how they look.*
- [ ] *(Homepage video: leave it — it doesn't affect the ads, which go to the booking page.)*

**Proof** *(cold visitors book when they see proof — there's none on the site yet)*
- [ ] **Get 2–3 short written testimonials** from current agency clients (CK&Co, Bayleys, Kelly&Co, MC Property Group, Voyle&Co) and add them to the booking page. *This is your homework — it's the biggest lever on whether clicks turn into bookings.*
- [ ] Add the phone number (022 506 8721) as a tap-to-call button in the top menu. *(Many agents will call instead of filling a form.)*

## Phase 2 — Build the campaign (in Google Ads)

- [ ] **One Search campaign only.** Ignore Google's prompts to use "Performance Max," "Smart" or "Display" — those waste small budgets.
- [ ] **Bidding:** start on **"Maximize Clicks" with a max bid of about NZ$6.** Switch to "Maximize Conversions" only after you've collected ~10–15 bookings.
- [ ] **Location:** Wellington, ~25km around the CBD (covers Lower/Upper Hutt, Porirua). Set to **"Presence — people in this location,"** NOT "interested in." Exclude the rest of NZ. *(This one setting prevents about a third of wasted spend.)*
- [ ] **Schedule:** weekdays ~7am–7pm + Saturday morning.
- [ ] **Keywords — use exact/phrase match only (never "broad match"),** split into 3 groups:
  - **Photography:** "real estate photography wellington", "real estate photographer wellington", "property photography wellington", "property photographer wellington"
  - **Drone:** "real estate drone photography wellington", "aerial property photography wellington", "drone photography wellington real estate"
  - **Video & specialty:** "real estate videographer wellington", "property video wellington", "virtual tour real estate wellington", "matterport wellington", "3d floor plan wellington"
- [ ] **Negative keywords (add day one):** jobs, course, free, salary, cheap, DIY, how to, camera, hire, rent, rental, wedding, family, portrait, Auckland, Christchurch, Hamilton, Tauranga.
- [ ] **Send all ads to the booking page (`book.html`)** — never the homepage.
- [ ] **Ad copy** — write 2 responsive ads using these angles:
  - Headlines: "Wellington Real Estate Photography" · "Next-Day Delivery, Guaranteed" · "Bundle & Save ~$290" · "A Team, Not a Solo Shooter" · "Aerial Photography From $175" · "Trusted by Bayleys & CK&Co"
  - Descriptions: lead with **next-day delivery**, **one local team / one invoice**, **licensed drone pilot on staff**, and the **full 5-service package ($980, save ~$290)**.
- [ ] Turn on all **ad extensions:** sitelinks (Portfolio, Services, Book, About), callouts (Next-Day Delivery, Licensed Drone Pilot, Wellington-Local), and a **call button** with 022 506 8721.
- [ ] **Budget pacing:** about **$15–18/day for 5–6 weeks.** Do NOT let it spend the $600 in a week — you need time to see which searches work and cut the rest.

## Phase 3 — After launch (check weekly, not daily)

- [ ] Open the **"Search terms" report** weekly. Add any irrelevant search as a negative keyword; if a term is producing bookings, give it its own keyword.
- [ ] Note which of the 3 keyword groups actually produces bookings, and your cost per booking.
- [ ] Watch how many people **call** vs. fill the form.
- [ ] **After the $600 is spent, the win is knowing the 2–3 keywords that produced bookings.** If a keyword's cost-per-booking is under ~$150, put $300–500/month behind only those. If search volume was just too thin (Wellington is a small market), don't pour in more search budget — invest the energy in referrals/LinkedIn/sample-reel drops to agencies instead.

---

## Decisions for the client to make

1. **Will you commit to Phase 1 first?** (Tracking + testimonials.) If not, the honest advice is **don't run the ads** — an untracked campaign teaches you nothing.
2. **Who sets up the Google Ads account and Cal.com redirect** — you, or your developer?
3. **Which 3 agencies will you ask for a testimonial,** and by when?
4. **Are you OK treating this as a $600 test** (expect 2–5 bookings), rather than a guaranteed return?

## The honest truth

$600 is about one Classic Photoshoot in revenue. Real estate agents — your main buyers — usually find photographers by **referral**, not by Googling, and Wellington is a small market. So it's genuinely possible paid search underperforms word-of-mouth for this business. That's not a reason to skip the test — it's the reason to **set it up properly** (tracking + testimonials) so that even a quiet result tells you something worth knowing. Set it up right, or don't run it.
